How it works

Every game night, PuckProb gives the probability that each player gets a shot, a goal, an assist or a point, each team's chance of winning, and the probs for every award and the Stanley Cup. Percentages only.

In beta

PuckProb is in beta: the models are still being fine-tuned and the track record fills up night after night. A number looks off? Write to us at contact@puckprob.ca, it helps.

Players

For each player: his shot, goal and assist rates per 60 minutes (weighted toward recent games and pulled toward the average when he has played little), his expected ice time, his power-play unit, the opponent (defense, starting goalie), home ice, back-to-backs, his own personal tendencies and advanced stats from MoneyPuck. With tonight's lineup, we adjust his line, his power-play role and the opposing goalie.

Each factor pushes his chances up (+) or down (−) compared to an ordinary game: in the app, tap “Why this %” under a player to see them one by one. Several models are compared continuously against real results; the one that predicts each category best is the one we publish.

Games

Each team's expected goals come from recent form, both teams' offense and defense, and the goalies. The win probability includes overtime and the shootout.

Awards and the Cup

The rest of the season is simulated 10,000 times. For voted awards (Hart, Norris, Vezina, Calder, Selke, etc.), we learned what voters reward from past winners. For the Selke, for example, reputation matters a lot.

Checked every night

Every probability is compared with the real result. It's all on the Track record page.

Sources

Public NHL data, advanced stats from MoneyPuck.com, lineups from DailyFaceoff. Independent site, not affiliated with the NHL or its teams. No official photos or logos are used.

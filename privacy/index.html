Privacy policy

What PuckProb collects (almost nothing), who receives what, and how to say no.

Who we are

PuckProb (puckprob.ca) is an independent website run from Quebec, Canada. Person in charge of the protection of personal information: the site owner, reachable at contact@puckprob.ca.

What we collect

No account, no sign-up form. We don't ask for your name or email. To count visits, we use GoatCounter (goatcounter.com, Netherlands): no cookies, no tracking across sites, and no personal data is kept. It only records anonymous totals such as pages viewed, the referring site, country, and screen size. If you write to us, we keep your message only as long as needed to answer it.

Who receives information when you visit

Hosting. The site is hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., United States), which receives your IP address and browser information in order to deliver the pages, like any web host.

Live scores. When the LIVE button is on, your browser may request game data directly from the NHL's public service (api-web.nhle.com), which then receives your IP address.

Fonts and images. Fonts are hosted on our own site; no official NHL photos or logos are loaded.

Advertising (Google AdSense)

To stay free, PuckProb shows ads from Google AdSense. Nothing related to ads is loaded until you click “Accept” in the banner. If you accept, Google uses cookies and similar technologies to choose ads (possibly personalized), limit how often you see them and measure them; this data is processed by Google outside Quebec, including in the United States. If you refuse, no ads are shown and no ad cookie is placed. You can change your choice at any time with the “Cookie settings” link at the bottom of every page. More information: how Google uses data in ads.

Share images

The “Share” buttons create the image right in your browser: nothing is sent to us. If you share it, the app you pick (Facebook, Messenger, your texts...) receives it under its own privacy policy.

Stored on your device

A few settings are saved in your browser's storage: the LIVE button, your team (“My team”) and your ad choice. They never leave your device, and clearing your browser data erases them.

Children

PuckProb is meant for a general adult audience. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

You can ask to access or correct personal information about you, or withdraw your consent, by writing to contact@puckprob.ca.

Last updated: November 7, 2026.

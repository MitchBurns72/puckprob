What's new

Everything that changed on PuckProb, newest first. Built by fans, for fans: here's what we've been up to.

V1.011 · November 7, 2026

New menu bar, Refs and every player's card

  • New bar at the bottom of the screen, thumb-friendly: Tonight, Races, Stats, Teams and "All". Each family's sections as chips at the top, and "All" opens every section of the site as tiles.
  • New Refs section: the players refs don't like, and their favorites. Penalties drawn and taken by every player, compared with a similar player in the same situations (leading, tied, trailing).
  • Every player's card (through his team's page) now shows his rank in every section: deserved salary, projections, duos and lines, Refs, Enforcers, GCR and What if.

V1.010 · November 7, 2026

Facebook, Instagram and scores that make sense

  • PuckProb is on Facebook and Instagram: 3 posts in the morning, afternoon games around noon, then the top 5 goals, assists and 3 shots plus one post per game at night. Every post gets a comment explaining why, with numbers.
  • New "Follow PuckProb" bar at the top of every page, and a puckprob.ca/liens page with the latest posts.
  • After the games, two "On the move" posts: the 10 players and the 10 teams that moved the most since the day before, each with the why in numbers.
  • A game's predicted score is now a real final score (overtime and empty nets included), tuned and checked on more than 14,000 real NHL games. It also shows what's special about each game, not just 3-2 every night.
  • Lineup names now match the NHL's: a player like Zachary Bolduc is no longer marked out by mistake.
  • Goalies have their number on their plate, and the small day-by-day curve follows the round picked in the Stanley Cup.
  • The small day-by-day curve is now everywhere: awards, Stanley Cup, projected standings, team and player pages, projections, salaries, Enforcers and GCR.
  • Over 5.5 and 6.5 goals % are recalibrated and checked on more than 8,000 games: they count the overtime goal and follow this season's scoring level.
  • PB SIM: after the Cup, the draft lottery (the NHL's real odds, on your simulated season's standings) and a mock draft with the top 2027 prospects. Goalies have their number and role everywhere.

V1.009 · November 7, 2026

Your team everywhere and real positions

  • With My team, the whole site takes your team's three colors and only those: backgrounds, buttons, tabs and the ticker. Phones in dark mode too.
  • Plate numbers are outlined in the team's third color: they stand out more.
  • Plates show tonight's real position: LD, RD, LW, C, RW, from the lineup.
  • Goalies have their role on the team: SGS (the starter, alone), SGA, SGB, SGC (starters sharing the net), BG (the backup), then 3G and 4G. Based on starts this season and in the last 10 games.

V1.008 · November 7, 2026

PB SIM, the goalie GCR and full units

  • New PB SIM tab, between Games and Trophies: play the rest of the season in front of you. The points chart draws live, then the playoffs are played game by game in a bracket (West on the left, East on the right, the final in the middle) all the way to the champion.
  • PB SIM is shareable: three images (the chart, your playoffs, the champions and records of your season) and a link that replays the exact same season today. The simulated standings also show goals for and against per game.
  • New GCR tab (Goalie Confidence Rating): the goalies everyone trusts the most. He stops more than expected, his team plays with confidence in front of him (the same players allow fewer dangerous chances when he's in net) and his coach trusts him.
  • Games page: under each of tonight's goalies, his GCR and how his team plays in front of him.
  • Duos: full 5-on-5 units (3 forwards and 2 defensemen), the power play (5-on-4, 5-on-3, 4-on-3), 4-on-4 and overtime. Always the same guys on the ice together.
  • Sharing a card now sends two images: the card, and the top 10 of the list it came from.
  • New night look: the site takes your team's colors (picked in My team), with its diagonal stripes like a jersey hem. No team: the PuckProb colors.
  • Cards go dark: the header in the team's colors with its stripes, the numbers in its color. Plates use each team's real colors: the background in its dark color, the number and the outline in its other colors (black and orange for the Flyers, blue, white and red for the Canadiens, navy and gold for the Predators).
  • The GCR and the units fill up with the new game harvest: a few updates.

V1.007 · November 7, 2026

Your team, images to share and duos

  • My team: pick your team once (at the top of every page). The home page shows “Your team tonight”: the game, your win prob, your 3 likeliest scorers and your Cup prob. Everywhere else, your team comes first. Saved on your device only, no account.
  • Share button: every player card and tonight's top 5 turn into a clean image, ready for Facebook, Messenger, Reddit or your group chat.
  • Simulate the rest of the season: every team now has its wins-losses-OT losses record and its goals for and against, from today's real standings to the last game.
  • Salaries: players traded since our contract list get their contract back with their new team.
  • Tonight: one single menu, the tab bar, with Teams, Track record, About, What's new and Home at the end (the logo also takes you home).
  • New Duos tab: teammates who are better together than apart, at 5-on-5. Duos and lines, ranked by chemistry, with what they do together and each one on his own. It fills up over the next updates.
  • Share everywhere in Awards: the full Stanley Cup ranking (East and West on one image), the projected final standings, the top 5 of every award, and even your simulated season with its champion.
  • Our own xG now runs everywhere on PuckProb. On the Track record page, a bar compares it with MoneyPuck on the goals actually scored: 100% = as good, over 100% = better.
  • Simulate the rest of the season: a chart now draws live during the simulation, every team's points day by day. Check or uncheck teams (all, none, East, West) to compare the ones you care about. The standings also show every team's longest winning streak (LWS) and losing streak (LLS).
  • Duos: new D pairs view. The best defense pair combinations, ranked by chemistry at 5-on-5.
  • Every update now has a version number (V1.001 for the beginnings, V1.007 today). Privacy policy updated: ads, share images and My team.
  • Duos: the big number on the right follows the chosen sort (chemistry, share together, minutes or goals together). In dark mode, the selected filter finally stands out.
  • Share the top 20: a button at the top of the list in Tonight, What if, Salaries, Enforcers, Projections, Duos and every award. The image follows the sort, filter and team you picked.
  • Fixed: tonight's players and games list could show up empty, live scores didn't update on the French site, and duo chemistry trusted small samples too much (it's now pulled toward average on both sides, together and apart).

V1.006 · November 6, 2026

Clearer, smoother

  • Changes since yesterday (spots gained ▲, spots lost ▼, + or − %) are now solid green or red pills: they jump out everywhere.
  • In cards (What if, Salaries, Enforcers, Projections), swipe left or right to jump to the next or previous player in the list, and down to close. On a computer: the arrow keys.
  • New What's new page: this one! Every update, with its date and what it changes.
  • Desktop version: on a big screen, the site uses the full width, lists go two columns and cards open in a side panel on the right, without hiding the list.
  • Salaries: real contracts for 12 more teams (Anaheim, Boston, Buffalo, Carolina, Columbus, Calgary, Chicago, Colorado, Dallas, Detroit, Edmonton, Philadelphia). Compound names (Nugent-Hopkins, O'Connor, Del Mastro…) are now matched.
  • Our own xG (expected goals) is being built from the NHL play-by-play. Its comparison with MoneyPuck will show up in the Track record once it has enough shots.

V1.005 · November 5, 2026

Projections, Badassness and the simulated season

  • New Projections tab: where every player will finish in goals, assists, points and penalty minutes.
  • In every projection card, the season chart: the end-of-season projection redone after each game, next to what actually happened.
  • Sharper projections: last season counts three times more than the one before, and a player who never misses games is no longer punished for a few absences.
  • “On the move” on the home page: the biggest move since yesterday in each section (trophies, Cup, standings, projections, salaries, enforcers, What if), also in the ticker.
  • Enforcers: new Badassness part. Fighting bigger guys, taking hits, blocking shots.
  • Stanley Cup: the Cup in charts, a pie for the chosen round and each team's staircase from the playoffs to the Cup.
  • Standings: “Simulate the rest of the season” button. One possible season played live, then the playoffs, all the way to the champion.

V1.004 · November 3, 2026

Real contracts and player plates

  • Salaries: real contracts for 20 teams, compared to what each player deserves. Three new sorts: Bargains, Overpaid and Return (what he brings as a % of what he costs).
  • Every player has a plate in his team's colors, with his number and position, everywhere on the site. Retired players in What if get their last number.
  • Enforcers: chaos. When he jumps on the ice, do things get rowdier? Fights, hits, penalties and scrums, compared to when he's on the bench.
  • Team filters in What if, Salaries and Enforcers.

V1.003 · November 2, 2026

Enforcers, daily changes and BETA

  • New Enforcers tab: the top 50 players the other team fears most, with an index out of 100 (the #1 at 100%).
  • Changes since yesterday, everywhere: green when it goes up, red when it goes down, plus spots gained or lost.
  • Track record: “better than guessing”, how much our probabilities beat chance (Brier score).
  • BETA label: the site is being fine-tuned, your feedback helps (contact@puckprob.ca).

V1.002 · November 1, 2026

puckprob.ca launches

  • Every night, each player's prob to score, get a point, an assist or shots, and every game's win probs.
  • The award races (Hart, Art Ross, Rocket Richard, Norris, Vezina, Calder and the rest) and the Stanley Cup, simulated 10,000 times.
  • All 32 teams, a page for every player and the Track record: every prediction checked after the game.
  • What if: all-time careers, era-adjusted, over 82 games.
  • Deserved salaries: what every player is really worth, with a scouting report.
  • The nightly ticker (streaks, returns, probs to watch).
  • In English and French: the FR | EN button switches language on any page.

V1.001 · October 2026

Before PuckProb: the beginning

  • It all starts with a fan's question: tonight, which players are most likely to get 2+ shots?
  • Then goals, assists and points (1+, 2+, 3+), every game's winner and total goals.
  • From day one, every prediction is checked after the game. Behind the %: our own machine-learning models, trained on full seasons and put back to the test every week against real results.
  • Live stats during games, and simulated award races and Stanley Cup.

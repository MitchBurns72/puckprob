Perreault goes down, the Rangers teeter, and Edvinsson looks to San Jose

By PuckProb.ca · November 27, 2026

The Rangers lose Gabe Perreault, a $0.9M bargain worth $5.2M by our PB Market Value, and slide to a 46.8% playoff prob. In Detroit, the Edvinsson file runs through San Jose, and Jeannot and Bauer pulled off the day's only real "trade."

Day after American Thanksgiving, and on paper the NHL served up a quiet one. Except in New York, where the turkey went down the wrong pipe. The Rangers lose Gabe Perreault to injury and recall Juuso Parssinen from Hartford (Pro Hockey Rumors). Looks like a small notebook move. It isn't.

The Rangers lose their bargain

Gabe Perreault has 7 goals and 11 points in 23 games. Nothing earth-shattering, but look at the price tag: our PB Market Value pegs him at $5.2M a year, while he costs the Rangers $0.9M. That's the kind of entry-level deal that makes a GM grin, and he's the one going down. Last week it was Oliver Bjorkstrand. That's a lot of bodies in the trainer's room for a team with no cushion.

Because there is no cushion. The Rangers slide to 17th in our Power rankings (-3 this week) and sit at a 46.8% prob of making the playoffs. Their 6-4-0 record over the last 10 is fine, nothing more. In our lineups, Parssinen slots straight onto the third line with Max Sasson and Taylor Raddysh. Honest work, but it doesn't replace a kid with 7 goals. My call: without Perreault, New York drops below 45% within a week. Not a disaster, but the kind of slide that turns into a mad scramble in March.

Edvinsson: it all runs through San Jose

The Simon Edvinsson saga rolls on, and according to The Hockey Writers, every road leads to San Jose: talks with the Red Wings reportedly hinge on a single Sharks prospect. Detroit has other suitors, but that's the sticking point. My take: Detroit can't afford to wait. The Wings are 22nd in our Power rankings (+1), with a 9.4% playoff prob and a 3-7-0 record over their last 10. When you've lost seven of ten, you're not negotiating from strength, you're negotiating with your fingers crossed.

And the Sharks? 30th, 6.0% for the playoffs. They're shopping for the future, not for April, and that makes sense. What makes less sense is the Maple Leafs showing up in the same sentence as Edvinsson (The Hockey Writers). Toronto is 26th (-1), 1-8-1 over its last 10 and at a 3.5% playoff prob. Come on. Before buying a defenceman, maybe win two in a row first. I'm filing that under "November rumour."

The only trade of the day

Our feed filed this under "trades," and technically it's right: Tanner Jeannot and Tyrel Bauer traded about forty punches over a full minute, one of the longest fights the NHL has seen in a while (RDS). By the numbers, Tanner Jeannot has 6 points in 23 games and a PB Market Value of $0.8M on a $3.4M contract. Boston isn't paying him for his soft hands, let's say. Tyrel Bauer: 2 points in 19 games. The Bruins stay 8th (74.8% playoff prob), and the Jets, 8-1-1 over their last 10, climb 3 spots to 16th. Winnipeg is the hot team nobody's talking about.

Quick hits

  • Edmonton: Matt Savoie is getting closer to a return (The Hockey Writers). The Oilers don't need him to make the playoffs (97.6%), but for the Cup (6.2%, 6th favourite), every piece counts.
  • The Xhekaj brothers in hot water, per the headline of the same column (The Hockey Writers). Arber Xhekaj already has 34 penalty minutes in 24 games. Shocking absolutely no one.
  • Montreal: back from surgery, Bryce Pickford is sent to the Laval Rocket (La Presse). The Habs, 12th (-2), sit at a 69.2% playoff prob.
  • Vancouver has plenty of pending UFAs to flip before the deadline (Daily Faceoff). At 13 points in 27 games, 32nd and 0.2% for the playoffs, that's called a garage sale.
  • Colorado: Gavin Brindley goes on injured reserve, Taylor Makar comes up from the AHL. Not Cale, Taylor. The Avalanche remain our 2nd Cup favourite at 13.1%, behind Carolina (25.0%).
  • Chicago recalls Dillon Boucher and sends Dominic Toninato to the AHL. The Hawks are 31st, 0.6% for the playoffs.

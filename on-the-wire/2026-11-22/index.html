MacKinnon out three months as Detroit's Larkin saga drags on

By PuckProb.ca · November 22, 2026

Colorado loses its engine for a lot longer than expected, Anaheim loses a $1M player worth ten times that, and in Detroit, the Larkin saga is closing in on a month. We lay out the timeline.

Let's start with the big one. According to RDS, Nathan MacKinnon's absence goes from about a month to three months. Ouch. We're talking 30 points in 20 games, a 123-point pace, and a PB Market Value of $17.9M on a $12.6M contract. In our trophy races, he was 5th for the Art Ross (3.0%) and 5th for the Hart (2.3%), already well behind Connor McDavid and Leon Draisaitl. The Avalanche are still our 2nd Cup favourite at 12.1%, behind Carolina (22.7%). But with Cale Makar also on injured reserve since Thursday, Colorado's infirmary now has a better top six than plenty of teams in this league.

Larkin gate: the timeline

In Detroit, this has turned into a soap opera. A quick look back, because it's getting long as heck:

  • October 29: the Red Wings suspend Dylan Larkin without pay after his decision to no longer play for the team. He had 6 points in 10 games.
  • November 20: The Hockey Writers lays it out: Larkin's trade request and the contract stalemate with Simon Edvinsson have the club in a real bind.
  • November 21: The Hockey Writers again reports an Edvinsson deal could be coming, with the December 1 deadline looming.
  • November 21: Daily Faceoff asks which teams need a trade to save their season. Detroit is on the list. Bonus: the piece is written by Matt Larkin. You can't make this up.

Since the suspension, the Wings have played 13 games, and they're 2-8-0 in their last 10. They sit 25th in our Power rankings with a 7.3% prob of making the playoffs. My take: every week this drags on, Detroit negotiates with a little less leverage. Larkin's PB Market Value ($9.1M) still tops his contract ($8.7M). The player keeps his value; it's the handling of the file that's losing it. It feels like a GTA 6 mission: everybody's looking for the way out of town, and nobody's found the car yet.

And what about Laine?

The question that keeps coming up: what's going on with Patrik Laine? Boring answer: nothing in our feed over the last 24 hours, and he doesn't show up anywhere in our numbers this season. That's quantum mechanics right there. Until you open the box, Laine is both about to come back and gone for good, like Schrödinger's cat. I don't know if I'm going completely crazy in the head or what, but we'll wait for real news to collapse the wave function before saying anything.

Anaheim loses its bargain

The Ducks put Beckett Sennecke on injured reserve and recall Nico Myatovic from San Diego (Pro Hockey Rumors). Bad news: 26 points in 22 games, 12 of them goals, and a PB Market Value of $10.7M on a contract of roughly $1M. Anaheim, 21st in our Power rankings (-3 this week) with a 32.8% prob of making the playoffs, loses a ridiculous bargain.

Quick hits

  • Quinn Hughes will miss at least two games and Minnesota recalls Viking Gustafsson Nyberg from Iowa (Pro Hockey Rumors). The Wild stay 3rd in our Power rankings, 8-0-2 in their last 10.
  • Matthew Knies says his return to Toronto feels a little awkward (Daily Faceoff). Awkward is also the word for the Leafs: 1-8-1 in their last 10, 28th in our Power rankings, 2.4% prob of making the playoffs. Come on.
  • Ottawa is reportedly eyeing Bryan Rust and Rickard Rakell (The Hockey Writers). Rakell: $7.1M PB Market Value, $5.0M contract.
  • The Kings could shop Drew Doughty (Daily Faceoff). PB Market Value: $1.6M. Contract: $11M. Good luck with that, gosh.
  • The Habs: Pro Hockey Rumors checks in on Alexandre Carrier, Zachary Bolduc and a forward returning to the lineup. Montreal is 10th in our Power rankings and the 9th Cup favourite (3.4%).

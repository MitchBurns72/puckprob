Where does the Kuch land? The Kucherov file, RNH's return and Binnington's benching

By PuckProb.ca · November 25, 2026

The Habs reportedly have an eye on Nikita Kucherov, 3rd in our Art Ross race: we open the file on his possible landing spots. Also: RNH returns in a loss to Vegas, and Joel Hofer has taken the net from Binnington in St. Louis.

Lots of chatter around Tampa this morning. According to The Hockey Writers, the Canadiens have an eye on Nikita Kucherov. The Kuch himself, no less. So we're doing what Mitch asked: opening the file and looking at where Kuchy could land, numbers in hand.

The Kuch file: where could he land?

First, a reminder of what's on the shelf. The Kuch has 32 points in 23 games (10 goals, 22 assists), on pace for 116. His PB Market Value is $17.0M, 5th in the whole league, on a $9.5M contract. He's 3rd in our Art Ross race (11.8%), 4th for the Hart (9.6%) and 2nd for the Ted Lindsay (13.1%), right behind McDavid. And the Lightning are 4th in our Power rankings, with a 93.6% prob of making the playoffs and 8.7% for the Cup. Come on, why would you sell that?

  • Montreal: the reported lead. The Habs are 11th in our Power rankings, 74.5% for the playoffs, 3.3% for the Cup. Adding Kuchy to a team that just landed Larkin would scream all-in. I love it, and so does my fan wallet.
  • Carolina: our big favourites at 23.9% for the Cup. The rich getting richer is boring, but on paper it's the most logical fit.
  • Colorado: 3rd favourite at 11.7%, but with Makar on injured reserve. If any team needs a Rough guy to hold the fort, it's them.
  • Toronto, for laughs: the Leafs are reportedly shopping Auston Matthews, per the same source. Matthews for Kucherov? The Leafs are 27th with a 3.5% playoff prob and a 1-8-1 record in their last 10. Tampa would have to lose its mind first.

My crazy prediction, and I'll own it: the Kuch doesn't budge an inch, he passes McDavid in the Ted Lindsay race, and the Habs fall back on a plan B before the holidays. Tampa doesn't trade its best player when he's 5th in the whole league in PB Market Value.

RNH is back, the Oilers didn't follow

Ryan Nugent-Hopkins was day-to-day over the weekend, a game-time decision yesterday, and he played: first line and first power-play unit. The result: one shot, no points, and a 5-3 loss to Vegas in Edmonton. He still has 11 points in 12 games this season, very solid production. The Oilers stay 5th in our Power rankings, 97.8% for the playoffs, 6.8% for the Cup. But The Hockey Writers notes that Zach Hyman's health is a worry in Edmonton. He has 6 points in 14 games. Not the time to lose bodies.

Binnington loses his net in St. Louis

In St. Louis, the debate is over, and our numbers make it pretty clear. Joel Hofer has played 17 games with a .919 save percentage and a PB Market Value of $14.2M, 14th in the whole league, on a $3.4M deal. Jordan Binnington: 7 games, .847, a PB Market Value of $0.85M on a $6M salary. Darn, that's a big gap. The Blues jumped 7 spots in our Power rankings this week (13th) and have a 60.4% playoff prob. My prediction: Hofer keeps the job until April. Meanwhile, St. Louis recalls Adam Jiricek to fill the holes left by injuries (Pro Hockey Rumors).

Quick hits

  • Per Elliotte Friedman, relayed by The Hockey Writers, an in-season Matthews trade remains very unlikely. His PB Market Value ($14.8M) still tops his contract ($13.2M).
  • Ottawa survived Brady Tkachuk's exit, and Daily Faceoff gives Travis Green the credit. The Sens are 9th (+2), 7-1-2 in their last 10.
  • Sidney Crosby wants to stay a Penguin forever, his agent says (Daily Faceoff).
  • La Presse looks back at the Dylan Larkin trade: Detroit (25th, 7.5% playoff prob) shifts into youth mode.
  • The Flyers are playing better, but a Rasmus Ristolainen trade remains likely (Daily Faceoff). PB Market Value of $3.1M on a $5.1M contract.
  • On injured reserve: Kyle Palmieri (Islanders) and Mathieu Olivier (Columbus).
  • Washington claims Rémi Poirier off waivers, for a 3rd time this season per RDS. The guy should rent an Airbnb by the month.
  • On waivers: Calvin Pickard (Pro Hockey Rumors) and Cole Schwindt (Pro Hockey Rumors).
  • The Habs recall Brett Berard and David Reinbacher from Laval. Ottawa recalls Djibril Touré and sends Carter Yakemchuk to Belleville (Pro Hockey Rumors).

For the random facts about a random player

Today, PB Stats drew Larry Playfair, a Sabres and Kings defenceman from 1978 to 1990: 688 games, 26 goals, 120 points. Fact of the day: the last 106 games of his career, spread over three seasons, ended with exactly zero goals. And in 1985-86 he was -14 in 14 games with Los Angeles. One per night, like clockwork. Respect anyway: 688 NHL games is 688 more than the rest of us.

Hagel and Makar go down: a rough day for the chasers

By PuckProb.ca · November 20, 2026

Tampa Bay and Colorado, our second and third Cup favourites behind Carolina, each put a big piece on injured reserve Thursday. Meanwhile, things are heating up in Detroit, and Pittsburgh is watching the train go by.

Let's start in Tampa. Brandon Hagel has 13 goals and 24 points in 20 games, an 84-point pace. Our PB Market Value says a player producing like that is worth $12.4M a year. He costs the Lightning $6.5M. That's one of the best contracts in the league, and he's the one going down. Jansen Harkins comes up from Syracuse to plug the hole.

Same day, same story in Colorado: Cale Makar lands on injured reserve after 23 points in 19 games, 18 of them assists (Pro Hockey Rumors). In our Norris race he was already 9th (1.6%), well behind Evan Bouchard (32%) and Quinn Hughes (28%), and a long absence won't help. The Avalanche are still 3rd in our Power rankings, with an 11.7% prob of lifting the Cup. The Lightning sit at 10.2%. Our simulations rerun every night: we'll know soon enough what these two absences really cost.

In Detroit, you can smell the smoke. A report relayed by Daily Faceoff says Simon Edvinsson turned down several offer sheets to force his way out, and The Hockey Writers points out that Dylan Larkin has asked out too. The Red Wings are 24th in our Power rankings with just a 9.9% prob of making the playoffs. Larkin only has 6 points in 10 games this season, but his PB Market Value ($9.1M) still tops his salary ($8.7M). For a buyer, that's not a bad deal.

In Pittsburgh, Sidney Crosby told RDS he's "in no rush to make a decision" about his future. Meanwhile, Nick Robertson goes on injured reserve with a rare stat line: 7 points in 20 games, all 7 of them goals. Zero assists. The Penguins, 22nd in our Power rankings, have a 9.6% prob of making the playoffs.

Quick hits

  • Calgary hopes to re-sign Morgan Frost (Daily Faceoff). Our PB Market Value puts him at $6.2M, up from $4.4M today. It's going to cost more.
  • Vegas puts Noah Hanifin on injured reserve and recalls Lukas Cormier.
  • Winnipeg places goalie Clay Stevenson on waivers (.862 SV%).
  • According to The Hockey Writers, Ottawa should trade Andre Burakovsky: 0 points in 8 games.
  • AHL shuffle: Pitlick (Minnesota), Silaev (New Jersey), Solberg (Anaheim), Biber (Utah).

Big quick hits: Hagel and Makar return, the clock ticks on Edvinsson

By Cloud Talbot · November 26, 2026

Big quick-hits edition: Hagel and Makar are back in action, the clock is ticking on Simon Edvinsson in Detroit, and we empty the notebook on a busy NHL week, PB numbers in hand.

Big quick-hits edition this morning. No deep dive, no novel: we're emptying the notebook from the week, one point at a time, short and almost serious. Grab a coffee, here we go.

The two comebacks

Six days ago, this wire opened with Brandon Hagel and Cale Makar on injured reserve. Full circle now: Tampa activated Hagel and Colorado did the same with Makar (Pro Hockey Rumors). Hagel: 24 points in 20 games, PB Market Value of $12.4M on a $6.5M deal. Makar: 23 points in 19 games, $11.5M in value. For an Avalanche team that has to make do without MacKinnon, it's pure oxygen: they stay our 2nd Cup favourite at 13.1%. My call: Makar climbs into the top 5 of our Norris race before Christmas. Seeing him 10th (1.5%) is about as natural as poutine without cheese curds.

Edvinsson: the clock is ticking

The next big soap opera is in Detroit again. According to The Hockey Writers, San Jose is pushing hard for Simon Edvinsson, with Edmonton also in the picture. Florida should be interested too, but don't expect a trade (The Hockey Writers). With the December 1 deadline coming, the Wings are walking a tightrope: 21st in our Power rankings (+2 this week), but only a 9.4% playoff prob. After Larkin, losing another core piece would sting like heck. My take: Detroit gets him signed, and the Sharks (30th, 6.5%) will have to shop elsewhere.

Quick hits: the week in a nutshell

  • Matthews: the same The Hockey Writers rundown puts the Rangers and Auston Matthews in the same sentence. 34 points in 26 games, 6th in our Rocket Richard race. The Leafs finally beat the Islanders (The Hockey Writers) and recalled goalie Artur Akhtyamov (Pro Hockey Rumors), but at a 3.5% playoff prob, one win is a drop in the lake.
  • Barkov and Marchand: Florida activates Aleksander Barkov and Brad Marchand. The Panthers move up to 11th (+1), 69.4% for the playoffs.
  • Ottawa: The Hockey Writers wonders whether the Sens, who survived Brady Tkachuk's departure, are real contenders. Our numbers: 10th, 7-1-2 in their last 10, 3.3% for the Cup. A polite contender, not a scary one yet.
  • MacKinnon: out three months instead of one, per RDS. The worst blow of the week: 30 points in 20 games, $17.9M PB Market Value.
  • Quinn Hughes on injured reserve (Pro Hockey Rumors). The Wild don't flinch: 3rd, 97.8% for the playoffs. Hughes stays 2nd in the Norris race (17.9%), behind Evan Bouchard (38%).
  • Bedard hurt (Pro Hockey Rumors). The Hawks won their last three anyway. At a 0.6% playoff prob, that's playing for pride.
  • St. Louis: Joel Hofer took the net from Jordan Binnington. A .912 save percentage, 4th in the Vezina race (6.9%). The Blues jumped 6 spots this week (14th), the biggest climb in the league.
  • Kucherov: the Habs rumour got people talking (The Hockey Writers). Nikita Kucherov is 3rd in the Art Ross race (11.3%). The Kuch stays in Tampa.
  • Montreal: Dylan Larkin has arrived, but the Habs lost their last three and slip to 12th (-2), 69.2% for the playoffs.
  • Edmonton: Ryan Nugent-Hopkins is back, but the Oilers are winless in four. Connor McDavid still has 59 points in 27 games and a 45.8% Art Ross prob. The rest of the league is playing for 2nd.
  • Rangers: Oliver Bjorkstrand hurt (Pro Hockey Rumors), Max Sasson arrives from Vancouver (Daily Faceoff). 16th (-2), 46.8%: right on the fence.
  • Pittsburgh: Sidney Crosby wants to be a Penguin forever (Daily Faceoff). The Pens lost their last five (27th, -5). Forever is a heck of a long time at 4.1%.
  • Los Angeles: the Kings are reportedly mulling a Drew Doughty trade (Daily Faceoff), $1.6M PB Market Value on an $11M deal. They recall Kirill Kirsanov (Pro Hockey Rumors) and climb 4 spots.
  • Remi Poirier: claimed by Washington for a 3rd time this season (RDS). More address changes than Montreal on July 1st.
  • Calder: Trevor Connelly (Vegas) is 2nd at 18.6%, behind Cole Hutson (41%). $5.8M PB Market Value on a $1.0M contract (Pro Hockey Rumors).
  • Vezina: Jesper Wallstedt leads our race at 40.2%. Nobody else is above 12%.
  • Vancouver: 13 points in 27 games, a 0.2% playoff prob. No joke, just our sympathies.
